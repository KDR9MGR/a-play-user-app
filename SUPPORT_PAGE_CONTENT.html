❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?
Download the A-Play app, tap "Sign Up", and enter your email and password. You can also continue as a guest to browse events without creating an account.
How do I book an event?
Browse events on the home screen, select your preferred event, choose your ticket zone and quantity, then proceed to payment. You'll receive a QR code ticket via email after successful payment.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept PayStack payments (Visa, Mastercard, mobile money) and Apple In-App Purchases for subscriptions. All payments are processed securely.
How do I reset my password?
On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot Password?", enter your email, and we'll send you a password reset link. Check your email (including spam folder) and follow the link to set a new password.
Can I cancel or refund my booking?
Cancellation policies vary by event. Generally, bookings can be cancelled up to 24-48 hours before the event for a refund. Check the specific event's cancellation policy before booking.
What is the Premium subscription?
Premium subscriptions (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum) give you access to exclusive features like concierge services, priority booking, reward points, and special discounts. Subscribe via the app's subscription page.
How do I use my QR code ticket?
After booking, you'll receive a QR code ticket in the app and via email. Simply show the QR code at the event entrance for scanning. Make sure to save it to your phone or print it out.
Can I browse events without signing in?
Yes! You can browse all events, clubs, and restaurants as a guest. Sign-in is only required when you want to book tickets, make reservations, or access premium features.
